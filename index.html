We work outside the traditional boundaries of wholesale tea procurement. Our search leads us to forgotten terraces, family-run mountainside plots, and untamed wild forests across remote growing regions.

Every harvest is treated as a unique record of weather, mineral density, and craft. By tasting hundreds of micro-lots each spring, we isolate selections that exhibit dramatic clarity and structured depth.
